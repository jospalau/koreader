Autumn ornament pack for Bookshelf (KOReader)
=============================================

12 autumn ornaments to sit on your shelves between your books. They are
drawn to suit both colour and black and white e-ink screens.

Installing
----------
1. Connect your e-reader to your computer.
2. Open the koreader folder, then icons, then bookshelf.ornaments.
   (On a Kindle this is /koreader/icons/bookshelf.ornaments.)
   If bookshelf.ornaments is not there yet, create it.
3. Copy the whole Autumn folder from this zip into bookshelf.ornaments,
   so you end up with bookshelf.ornaments/Autumn/Owl.png and so on.
4. On the e-reader, open the Bookshelf menu and go to
   Background and colours > Ornaments.
5. Tap the Autumn tab and make sure the pack is switched on.

Ornaments stand in the gaps on spine shelves. From the Ornaments screen
you can tap any ornament to switch it off or on, or switch the whole pack
off and on with the button at the bottom.

Needs Bookshelf v5.2 or later (earlier versions do not read ornament packs).

Illustrations are AI generated and hand tweaked.
